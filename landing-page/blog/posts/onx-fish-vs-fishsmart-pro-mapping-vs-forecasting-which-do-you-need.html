onX Fish vs FishSmart Pro: Mapping vs Forecasting (Which Do You Need?)

Two Good Apps That Do Completely Different Things

onX Fish has been making moves. In just the last few months, they've expanded from 2 states to 12, added a premium tier at $34.99/year, and built out an impressive lake discovery and mapping platform backed by onX's deep resources and DNR data partnerships.

If you're comparing onX Fish to FishSmart Pro, you're in the right place. I built FishSmart Pro, so I'm obviously not neutral — but I'll give you an honest breakdown because these two apps are designed for fundamentally different jobs. Understanding that difference will tell you which one you actually need.

Here's the short version: onX Fish helps you find a lake. FishSmart Pro tells you if the fish will bite when you get there. They're complementary tools, not direct substitutes.

Let me explain why.

What onX Fish Does Well

onX Fish is built on the same foundation that made onX Hunt and onX Backcountry successful: detailed mapping, property data, and offline GPS navigation. For fishing, that translates into:

For an angler who wants to discover new water, plan a trip to an unfamiliar lake, or navigate on the boat with GPS — onX Fish is genuinely strong. The DNR data integration is something no other fishing app offers at this depth.

The Key Difference: Discovery vs Prediction

Here's the simplest way to think about it:

onX Fish answers: "Where should I go fishing?"
- Which lakes have the species I want?
- How do I navigate this water?
- What does the structure look like underwater?
- What are the regulations here?

FishSmart Pro answers: "Will the fish bite when I get there?"
- Is the pressure trend triggering feeding?
- What's the actual water temperature from USGS data?
- Is dissolved oxygen adequate at the thermocline?
- What lure does the science match to these conditions?
- When are the peak feeding windows today?

These are two different stages of the fishing trip planning process. You use onX Fish to find the lake. You use FishSmart Pro to time your trip and choose your lure.

Honest Assessment: onX Fish's Strengths and Weaknesses

I want to be fair here because onX Fish is a well-built product:

Genuine strengths:
- The Lake Finder with DNR data is genuinely useful — no other app offers species/size/abundance filtering from state agencies at this depth.
- Offline 3D mapping is excellent for boaters who need GPS navigation.
- CarPlay integration is a real advantage for anglers with modern boat electronics.
- The 12-state coverage is growing rapidly and covers a huge chunk of the Midwest.

What it doesn't do:
- No bite prediction. No forecasting engine. No science-based activity score.
- No lure recommendations.
- No live water temperature integration.
- No transparent reasoning — you get data, but not analysis.
- No 12-hour feeding window prediction.

If you want to know whether it's worth driving to the lake right now, onX Fish can't tell you. It can tell you the lake exists and has your target species — but not whether conditions favor a bite today.
