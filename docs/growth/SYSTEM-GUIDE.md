# FishSmart Pro - Autonomous Growth System Guide

> **Your organic growth marketing engine is now running.**

---

## What Was Built

An autonomous growth system that runs on a weekly cadence:

1. **Research happens automatically** - competitor monitoring, trend tracking, keyword research, community pulse
2. **Content is drafted automatically** - blog posts, social media, Reddit posts, email newsletters, influencer outreach
3. **Everything goes to a review queue** - you approve before anything gets published
4. **You are notified only when needed** - publishing approvals, credentials, opportunities, or strategic decisions

---

## Your Weekly Rhythm

| Day | What Happens | What You Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday 8AM** | Research agent gathers intelligence | Nothing - fully automatic |
| **Tuesday 9AM** | Blog post + social media drafted | Review drafts in `review/blog/` and `review/social/` |
| **Wednesday 9AM** | Reddit + Facebook posts drafted | Review drafts in `review/reddit/` and `review/social/` |
| **Thursday 9AM** | Influencer outreach drafted | Review messages in `review/influencer/`, then send them |
| **Friday 9AM** | Email newsletter drafted | Review draft in `review/email/`, then send to your list |
| **1st of Month 10AM** | Strategy review + dashboard update | Review metrics, approve strategy adjustments |

---

## How to Review Content

1. Check the `docs/growth/review/` folders for new drafts
2. Read and edit anything you want to change
3. Approve by moving to `docs/growth/pipeline/` (ready to publish)
4. Publish on your platforms (Reddit, Facebook, email tool, blog)
5. After publishing, move to `docs/growth/published/` and add the URL
6. The Growth Dashboard tracks everything

## Philosophy

This system follows the strategy from your market analysis: **organic, content-led, word-of-mouth growth.** No paid ads. No spam. Just genuinely useful content that builds authority, trust, and awareness in the fishing community.

Every piece of content teaches something real about fishing science, mentions FishSmart Pro naturally, and respects the angler community's intelligence.
