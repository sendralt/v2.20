## TikTok Production Notes

### Batch Filming Tips

- Film ALL 7 videos in 1-2 sessions. Wear the same outfit (continuity doesn't matter on TikTok).
- Film outdoors when possible — natural light + fishing environment = credibility.
- Use a phone tripod or prop. Shaky footage kills retention.
- Screen-record the app segments separately, then edit them in with CapCut (free).

### Caption & Hashtag Formula

**Every video:**
- 1-line caption that complements (not repeats) the hook
- 4-6 hashtags: mix of broad (#fishing) and specific (#bassfishing, #fishingforecast)
- CTA: "Link in bio"

### Posting Cadence

| Week | Videos | Strategy |
|------|--------|----------|
| Week 1 (Launch) | 1 per day (7 total) | Maximum blitz — all formats |
| Week 2 | 5 videos | 2 forecast reveals, 2 science explainers, 1 story |
| Week 3+ | 3-5 per week | Mix of formats. Double down on what performs best. |

### TikTok Bio

> FishSmart Pro — Fishing forecasts powered by real science, not guessing. Live USGS data. Transparent bite scores. Zero social BS.
> [Link to app]

### Key Trends to Leverage

- Duets: If someone shares a catch, duet with a forecast showing why the bite was good
- Stitches: Stitch fishing tip videos and add the science angle
- Trending audio: Use for B-roll fishing footage with text overlays about the science
