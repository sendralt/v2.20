### Template: TikTok Comment Strategy (Alternative to DM)

Instead of cold DMing, engage first:

1. **Follow them** and like 3-5 of their recent videos
2. **Leave a genuine comment** on their latest video about the technique or fish shown (don't mention your app)
3. **Wait 2-3 days**, then DM with: "Hey, loved your [video] — I commented the other day. I built a fishing app and think you'd genuinely like it. Free Pro, no strings. Interested?"

This converts MUCH better than cold DMs.

## How to Find the Right Creators

### Search Terms (YouTube)
- "bass fishing tips" + filter: channel size 5K-50K
- "kayak fishing" + filter: channel size 5K-50K
- "fishing conditions explained"
- "barometric pressure fishing"
- "fishing gear reviews" (they review apps too)
- "how to fish for [species]"

### Search Terms (TikTok)
- #fishingtips
- #bassfishing
- #kayakfishing
- #fishinglife
- #fishingtutorial

### Target Criteria
- **Sweet spot: 5K-50K followers/subscribers** — big enough to have an engaged audience, small enough to respond to DMs
- **Content style:** Educational, tips, conditions-focused (not just catch montages)
- **Geography:** US-based (your data is US freshwater)
- **Vibe:** Authentic, science-curious, not overly sponsored

### Red Flags (Skip These)
- Excessive sponsored content (they'll expect payment)
- Saltwater/marine focused (wrong audience)
- Pure entertainment/comedy (audience won't convert)
- No contact info and closed DMs (can't reach them)
