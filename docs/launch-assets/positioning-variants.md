## Variant C — AI Directories

**Targets:** There's An AI For That, Futurepedia, Toolify, Future Tools, aitools.inc, AIStage, TopAI.tools, Good AI Tools, NewTools.site

**Tagline:**
> AI-explained fishing forecasts backed by real science.

**Long description (~150 words):**
FishSmart Pro is an AI-powered fishing forecast app that explains WHY fish will bite. A deterministic 10+ factor scoring engine calculates the bite from real environmental data — live USGS water temperature, barometric pressure trends with species-specific sensitivity, dissolved oxygen (Benson & Krause solubility), spawning cycle detection, thermocline depth, lunar phase, and civil-twilight photoperiod — and only then does Google Gemini translate it into a plain-language forecast.

What makes it AI-first:
- Engine-first, AI-second architecture — the LLM explains the science, it never generates the forecast
- AI lure recommendations merged with engine-matched picks, each source-tagged
- Transparent factor breakdown on every score, with confidence band
- 25 species-specific biological models
- 12-hour activity forecast with peak feeding windows

Built on Node.js with a deterministic EMA-smoothed engine, USGS and OpenWeatherMap live data, and Gemini AI enhancement. Use cases: trip timing, lure selection, learning fish behavior.

Free tier: 3 full AI forecasts per device. No signup for the free Bite Score Checker.
