# FishSmart Pro — Faceless YouTube Demo Video Script

**Video title:** FishSmart Pro — The Fishing App That Shows You WHY Fish Bite (Full Demo)
**Version referenced:** v2.22
**Target length:** 6-8 minutes
**Format:** 100% faceless — screen recordings, voiceover narration, animated text overlays, motion graphics, and B-roll. No on-camera presence at any point.

---

## Pre-Production Checklist

### Recording Setup
- [ ] **Screen capture tool:** OBS Studio (free) for web PWA capture at 1920×1080 or higher. Record at 60fps for smooth scroll playback.
- [ ] **Phone screen recorder** (optional alt): Android native screen recorder at 1080p+ for mobile app demo segments
- [ ] **Voiceover:** Record V.O. separately using a USB mic (Samson Q2U or similar) into Audacity (free). Process with noise reduction, slight compression, normalize to -3dB. Deliver at a conversational, confident pace.
- [ ] **Do Not Disturb:** Disable all notifications on phone and desktop before recording

### Pre-Planned Demo Data
- [ ] Pick a real freshwater location that produces a strong forecast (score 75+). Suggested: Lake Champlain, NY or Lake Okeechobee, FL
- [ ] Pre-select: **Largemouth Bass** as the demo species
- [ ] Pre-set water clarity to **Stained**
- [ ] Verify USGS data loads for the chosen location before recording
- [ ] Have at least 3-4 completed forecasts in history for the history/export segment

### Visual Assets to Prepare
- FishSmart Pro logo (transparent PNG)
- Animated title cards (lower-thirds style)
- Competitor pricing screenshots: Fishbrain ($59.99/yr), Navionics ($49.99/yr)
- B-roll clips: drone lake footage, sunrise over water, fishing rod/reel close-ups, lure tackle box, USGS stream gauge station, weather radar animation
- Icon/motion graphics: magnifying glass zoom icon, highlight box, green checkmarks, source badge mock-ups

### Editing Notes
- **Editor:** DaVinci Resolve (free), Premiere Pro, or Final Cut Pro
- **Export:** 1920×1080 (or 3840×2160 for 4K), 30fps minimum, H.264 MP4
- **Music:** Low-volume instrumental underneath V.O. — lo-fi or light electronic. Ducks fully during key data reveals.
- **Color treatment:** Clean, natural colors — no heavy grading on screen recordings
- **Zoom direction:** When script says "zoom in," use a smooth 1.5x-2x digital zoom with ease-in/out on the key UI element

## Production Notes

### Voiceover Direction
- **Tone:** Confident, knowledgeable, conversational — like an experienced angler explaining something to a friend
- **Pace:** Moderate for explainer segments (Segments 1, 3), slightly faster for demo segments (Segments 2, 6)
- **Energy:** Build from curious/frustrated in the cold open to confident/enthusiastic by the CTA
- **Pauses:** Let key data points breathe — when a number appears on screen (82/100, 91% match, $29.99/yr), pause for 0.5-1 second so the viewer can absorb it

### Screen Recording Tips
- Record at 2x the resolution you'll export at (e.g., record 4K, export 1080p) to allow clean digital zoom without pixelation
- Use a smooth-scrolling plugin or slow down scroll speed in editing — fast jerky scrolls look amateur
- Add a subtle cursor highlight (yellow circle) so viewers can follow where to look
- For the mobile app demo, use a phone mockup frame in the editor for polish

### B-Roll Insertion Points
- **0:00–0:03:** Competitor app screenshot (blurred)
- **1:10–1:20:** None (all motion graphics)
- **5:48–5:58:** USGS stream gauge station photo/video
- **6:40–6:48:** Competitor pricing screenshots
- Optional: Insert 1-2 second lake/sunrise B-roll clips between major segments as visual breathers

### Music Notes
- **Track style:** Light electronic or lo-fi instrumental. Avoid vocals — they compete with V.O.
- **Volume:** -20dB to -25dB under V.O. Duck to -30dB during data reveals and key numbers
- **Transitions:** Use music beats as natural cut points between segments
